Privacy Policy — Lary Publisher

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Lary Publisher is a personal tool made and used only by Lary Reyes to publish his own videos to his own social media accounts (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and Bluesky). It is not offered to the public.

What it uses: when Lary connects his own account, the platform gives the app a login token and basic profile information (name, username). The app uses these only to upload and publish the videos Lary chooses.

Where it is kept: everything stays on Lary's own computer. Login tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.

Other people: the app does not collect any information about other people.

Removing access: access can be removed at any time in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions). Removing it stops the app immediately.
